# Privacy Policy

Podshift is a personal app used only by its owner.

It reads the owner's WHOOP recovery, sleep, and cycle data solely to adjust that same person's Eight Sleep Pod temperature before bed. It does not sell data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising.

OAuth tokens and credentials stay on the owner's machine. The owner can revoke access at any time from the WHOOP app or the WHOOP developer dashboard.
